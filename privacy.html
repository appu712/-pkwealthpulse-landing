Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

FINUFF is a desktop application that keeps your family's financial records on your own computer. This policy sets out, plainly, what remains with you and what does not. For any question, write to support@finuff.com.

What remains on your computer

Everything you enter or import into the application (holdings, accounts, policies, loans, budget and notes) is stored on your own computer and encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is derived from your Master Password using Argon2id. We operate no server that stores your records; we therefore cannot see, open or recover them.

What the application transmits

  • Public market lookups. To display prices, fund NAVs and exchange rates, the application queries public data providers for a ticker, ISIN, fund code or currency pair. It does not transmit your holdings, balances or amounts.
  • License and device verification. To validate your license, the application sends your license key, email and name. To enforce the two-device limit, it also sends a one-way hash of a hardware identifier (never the identifier itself) and any device nickname you have chosen.
  • Alerts, only if you enable them. If you configure email, SMS or WhatsApp alerts, the text of the alert itself (a renewal date, for example) is passed to the alert service you selected. Your portfolio is not.
  • AI extraction, only if you enable it. This feature is off by default. If you add your own Claude (Anthropic) or Gemini (Google) API key and turn it on, the text or images of a document you choose to process are sent to that provider under your key. They are not sent to us.

The application contains no analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting service or advertising.

This website

  • finuff.com is hosted on Cloudflare, which maintains ordinary server logs. We run no analytics and set no cookies of our own.
  • The family-letter tool runs entirely in your browser. What you type is not sent to us.
  • The Microsoft Store badge is an image loaded from Microsoft.

The license request form

When you request a license, the form collects your name, email, mobile number, chosen license, computer type, a verification phrase and your date of birth. The form sends your details directly from your browser to Zoho Forms, where our license-request register is kept. We use these details solely to verify and issue your license and to assist you if you require license support. We do not use them for marketing.

Your password and recovery

Because we never hold your vault or Master Password, we cannot reset it. If both your password and your Recovery Code are lost, the data cannot be recovered. Keep an encrypted backup, and let your family know where it is kept.

Revisions

Should this policy change, the date above will change with it.
