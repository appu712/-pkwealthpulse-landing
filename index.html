How do I import my CAMS or KFintech CAS into a portfolio tracker?

Download your Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) from MF Central, CAMS or KFintech as a text or PDF file, then drop it into PK WealthPulse's Import screen. It reads the portfolio summary — fund name (AMFI-verified), folio number, cost value, market value and unit balance — directly from the statement, so you don't retype anything from your mutual fund holdings.

Can I track NSDL/CDSL demat holdings alongside mutual funds?

Yes. Broker contract notes and demat statements (NSDL and CDSL, including bonds and NCDs held as DEBT-sector rows) import the same way as CAS files, so your stocks, ETFs and mutual funds sit in one combined portfolio view instead of separate apps.

Is there a family finance tracker that doesn't upload data to the cloud?

PK WealthPulse stores every record — investments, bank accounts, loans, credit cards, insurance policies — locally on your Windows PC. There is no server sync and no cloud account behind your financial data, which matters if you'd rather not hand consolidated statements of your entire family's net worth to a third-party server.
