The Core Commitment

We don't receive, store, or have access to your portfolio or vault data. There is no backend that stores your portfolio, no account team that can query it, and no server-side copy for us to recover. WealthPulse may contact allowlisted market and currency providers for prices and exchange rates — those requests contain only the information required for the lookup, such as a ticker, ISIN or currency pair, not your portfolio, balances or vault contents. ₹1,999/year covers the whole household, not per user or per account — about ₹5.48 a day for the entire household. It covers every mutual fund, stock, bond, insurance policy, loan, EPF and gold holding your family tracks. Your Master Password is the only key that unlocks your data, derived on your own device, and it never leaves it. The trade-off: if you lose your device and your encrypted backup, we cannot recover your vault for you — that's the cost of keeping the data under your control instead of ours.

Encryption at rest AES-256-GCM

Your financial records at rest — holdings, balances, and policy details — are encrypted using AES-256-GCM for confidentiality and tamper detection. A small set of license-identity fields (name, email, license status), needed to verify the installation before your Master Password is entered, are kept in a separate local index by design.

Master key derivation Argon2id

Your encryption key is derived from your own Master Password using Argon2id, not from guessable identifiers such as your name or ID. Argon2id is designed to make large-scale password guessing substantially more expensive.

Local-first, no vendor copy

No cloud backend for portfolio storage. Your portfolio data remains on your own machine. License verification does not receive portfolio data, and there is no vendor-side portfolio copy.

No tracking. No telemetry. No ads.

No analytics SDK, crash-reporting service, or tracking pixel of any kind. Network calls are limited to allowlisted market/currency lookups, license verification, and — only if you opt in — the alert channel you configure.

Don't Take Our Word For It

Every claim above is something you can check yourself, not just something we assert. Open your browser or OS network monitor while using the app — outbound calls are limited to allowlisted market/currency lookups, license verification (which checks only your license key, email and name — never portfolio data), and, only if you've opted into email/SMS/WhatsApp alerts, the alert service you configured (which receives the alert text itself, e.g. a renewal date — never your full portfolio). The app's Content-Security-Policy ships as an explicit allowlist, not a default-open one, so there's no wildcard hiding an undisclosed destination. There is no PK WealthPulse financial account to create — license verification checks a license key, not a password, and no server on our end ever holds your financial data.

Your Master Password Is Not Recoverable

Because PK WealthPulse never possesses your vault or Master Password, we cannot reset your password or recover an encrypted vault if you lose both your password and backup. Keep your Master Password safe and maintain an encrypted backup — that's the trade-off for a financial vault with no vendor-side copy to fall back on.

Why We Don't Estimate Everything

WealthPulse deliberately doesn't turn uncertain valuations into precise-looking numbers. Real estate, private investments and crypto can involve valuation, liquidity, ownership and tax complexities that don't fit the same transparent, auditable calculation model used for your listed and statement-backed assets. We'd rather leave a valuation out than give you a precise-looking number we can't defend. It's a scope decision, not a missing feature.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I import my CAMS or KFintech CAS into a portfolio tracker?

Download your Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) from MF Central, CAMS or KFintech as a text or PDF file, then drop it into PK WealthPulse's Import screen. It reads the portfolio summary — fund name (AMFI-verified), folio number, cost value, market value and unit balance — directly from the statement, so you don't retype anything.

Does PK WealthPulse work on Windows, macOS and Linux?

Windows is available now for instant download from the Microsoft Store, as a code-signed build. macOS and Linux builds are produced on request — pick your platform in the license request form below and we'll follow up once it's ready.

Can I track NSDL/CDSL demat holdings alongside mutual funds?

Yes. Broker contract notes and demat statements (NSDL and CDSL, including bonds and NCDs held as DEBT-sector rows) import the same way as CAS files, so stocks, ETFs and mutual funds sit in one combined portfolio view instead of separate apps.

What license types are available and what do they cost?

Trial (14 days) is free. Pro (365 days) is ₹1,999/yr, Max (2 years) is ₹2,999 — for the entire household, not per user. That's about ₹5.48 a day for the whole household — to keep your family's financial details in your own hands instead of a vendor's server. Payment details for Pro/Max are shared by email once your request is verified. Verified requests get a response within 24 hours on weekdays, 48 hours on weekends; if it's taking longer than that, drop an email to pkwealthpulse@gmail.com and we'll sort it out.

Is there a family finance tracker that doesn't upload data to the cloud?

PK WealthPulse stores every record — investments, bank accounts, loans, credit cards, insurance policies — locally on your Windows PC. There is no server sync and no cloud account behind your financial data, which matters if you'd rather not hand consolidated statements of your entire family's net worth to a third-party server.

How does device activation work — can I use one license on multiple devices?

A license can be registered on up to 2 devices, but only one is ever active at a time. Logging in on your other registered device instantly makes it active and logs the first one out — no limit hit, no support needed. Trying a genuine 3rd device prompts you to replace one of the two registered slots. If you ever get stuck, email pkwealthpulse@gmail.com for help.

Why doesn't PK WealthPulse track real estate or crypto?

PK WealthPulse intentionally avoids presenting uncertain valuations as facts. Assets such as real estate and certain private/crypto holdings carry substantial valuation, liquidity, ownership and tax complexity. Rather than inventing a precise-looking number, the app currently limits its calculated wealth view to assets where the value can be reasonably explained and reproduced — mutual funds, stocks, global equities/ETFs, bonds, gold, bank accounts, deposits, loans, credit cards, insurance and subscriptions. It's a scope decision, not a missing feature.

What happens if I lose my device or Master Password?

WealthPulse cannot recover a vault it does not possess. If you lose your device, your encrypted backup is the recovery path — restore it on a new install with your Master Password. If you lose both your Master Password and access to a usable backup, the vault cannot be recovered by PK WealthPulse. That's the trade-off for a vault with no vendor-side copy to fall back on — see Profile → Encrypted Backup & Restore.

Does WealthPulse send my portfolio to market-data providers?

No. A live price/NAV lookup sends only the identifier needed for that lookup — a ticker, ISIN, fund code or currency pair — to the relevant public provider (AMFI, MoneyControl, BSE, Yahoo Finance, and others). It never sends your quantity, cost basis, balances, or any other part of your portfolio or vault.
