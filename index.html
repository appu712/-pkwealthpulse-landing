Encryption at rest AES-256-GCM

Every record — holdings, balances, policy details, personal identifiers — is encrypted with the authenticated-encryption standard approved for classified government use. Tampering is detectable, not just unreadable.

Master key derivation Argon2id

Your key is never derived from guessable identifiers like name or ID — it comes from your own Master Password via Argon2id, built to resist GPU/ASIC-accelerated brute-force attacks.

Local-first, zero-knowledge

No cloud backend for portfolio storage. Every encrypted record lives solely on your own machine — nothing to subpoena, breach, or monetize at our end, because it never exists there.

No tracking. No telemetry. No ads.

No analytics SDK, crash-reporting service, or tracking pixel of any kind. The only network calls are the allowlisted market/currency lookups your own portfolio needs.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I import my CAMS or KFintech CAS into a portfolio tracker?

Download your Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) from MF Central, CAMS or KFintech as a text or PDF file, then drop it into PK WealthPulse's Import screen. It reads the portfolio summary — fund name (AMFI-verified), folio number, cost value, market value and unit balance — directly from the statement, so you don't retype anything.

Does PK WealthPulse work on Windows, Mac and Linux?

Windows is available now for instant download from the Microsoft Store. Mac and Linux builds are produced on request — pick your platform in the license request form below and we'll follow up once it's ready.

Can I track NSDL/CDSL demat holdings alongside mutual funds?

Yes. Broker contract notes and demat statements (NSDL and CDSL, including bonds and NCDs held as DEBT-sector rows) import the same way as CAS files, so stocks, ETFs and mutual funds sit in one combined portfolio view instead of separate apps.

What license types are available and what do they cost?

Trial (14 days) is free. Pro (365 days) is ₹1,499/yr, Max (2 years) is ₹2,499 — payment details for Pro/Max are shared by email once your request is verified. Verified requests get a response within 24 hours on weekdays, 48 hours on weekends; if it's taking longer than that, drop an email to pkwealthpulse@gmail.com and we'll sort it out.

Is there a family finance tracker that doesn't upload data to the cloud?

PK WealthPulse stores every record — investments, bank accounts, loans, credit cards, insurance policies — locally on your Windows PC. There is no server sync and no cloud account behind your financial data, which matters if you'd rather not hand consolidated statements of your entire family's net worth to a third-party server.

How does device activation work — can I use one license on multiple devices?

A license can be registered on up to 2 devices, but only one is ever active at a time. Logging in on your other registered device instantly makes it active and logs the first one out — no limit hit, no support needed. Trying a genuine 3rd device prompts you to replace one of the two registered slots. If you ever get stuck, email pkwealthpulse@gmail.com for help.
