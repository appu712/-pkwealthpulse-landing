Security

Your financial data stays yours

Every design choice assumes your computer could be lost, stolen or inspected. The data on it stays meaningless without the one credential only you hold.

EncryptionAES-256-GCM, which also detects tampering
Your keyMade from your Master Password with Argon2id, never from your name or email
Where it livesOn your computer only. No vendor server holds a copy
TrackingNone. No analytics, telemetry or ad SDKs
Auto-lockAfter 5 minutes idle
RecoveryA one-time Recovery Code at setup. Keep an encrypted backup too
What does leave your computerPublic market lookups (a ticker, ISIN or currency pair), the license check (your license key, email and name) and, only if you turn them on, the alerts you set up (just the alert text, such as a renewal date). Never your portfolio, balances or vault.

We never hold your vault or Master Password, so we cannot reset it for you. If you lose both your password and your Recovery Code, the data cannot be recovered. Keep a backup.

Don't take our word for it: watch your computer's network monitor while you use FINUFF.

Why I built FINUFF
You spend years building your family's wealth. Make sure your family knows it exists.
1Money is scatteredStocks, funds, NPS, banks, FDs, property, insurance and loans.2It is rarely spoken aboutThe person managing it often never tells the family.3It is lost when neededIf they are unavailable, the family starts from scratch.

It started with my brother Rajesh. He invested for his family, and then he was no more. His loss showed me how much a family depends on one person knowing where everything is, and how little of it is written anywhere the rest of the family can find.

The person who needs the information most is often the person who never managed the money. So I built one private record for the whole household: on your own computer, encrypted, with nothing sent to me.

My own family now knows FINUFF exists, where the backup is kept, and how to open it. That is all this app is for.

    Questions

    Things people ask first

    Does FINUFF upload my data?

    No. Your records are stored encrypted on your own computer. The app only looks up public prices and fund NAVs; your holdings are not sent anywhere.

    Do I need to give my bank login?

    No. You import statements you already have, such as CAS, broker reports, bank statements and insurance PDFs.

    Is this investment advice?

    No. FINUFF is a record-keeping tool. It is not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser or Research Analyst.

    What if I forget my password?

    Your data is encrypted with your password. At setup, FINUFF also gives you a one-time Recovery Code. Keep both where your family can find them.
